How to export your chat history
To export your chat history from a chat:
Open the chat.
Tap More options > More > Export chat.
Tap Without media or Include media.
Choose your desired method (ex. Message, Mail, Add to Notes) to export chat history.
Note:
If you choose Include media, the most recent media sent will be added as attachments to be sent from your sharesheet.
Your chat history can’t be re-imported because it’s a text file and not a backup file.
