Can't back up or restore your chat history
Having access to your chats is crucial. We recognize that they contain valuable information and cherished memories, and we understand how distressing it can be to lose them, or accidentally delete them.
Note: Unfortunately, we can't restore your chats for you because your chat history isn’t stored on our servers.
Backup stuck
If your backup gets stuck while creating or restoring a backup, try:
Restart your device.
Log out and back into your Google Account.
Turn your Wi-Fi off and on, try connecting to a different Wi-Fi network, or try using mobile data.
Note: Switching between different Wi-Fi networks can cause the backup to timeout.
Reduce the size of your backup as your device might be running too low on space. Learn how here.
Check that your device’s battery is fully charged or plugged into a power source.
Force stop or close WhatsApp.
If you’re using a dual SIM phone, make sure the SIM connected to your WhatsApp phone number is active.
Check if the Google Play Services app needs to be updated.
Open your device Settings > tap Apps > Google Play Services.
Scroll to the bottom. Tap App Details > Update.
If you're still having trouble, try the following: Uninstall WhatsApp, restart your phone, and then reinstall WhatsApp from the Google Play Store.
Error messages
If you receive any of the following error messages:
‘This account already exists on your device.’
'Unable to access your account at this time.'
'Unable to back up messages.'
‘Couldn’t complete backup. Tap to give WhatsApp permission to back up to Google storage.’
Or your listed Google Account is grayed out when trying to add your Google Account to your WhatsApp.

Follow these steps to resolve error messages:
Check your WhatsApp permissions and make sure Photos and videos permission is set to Allow.
Go to your device Settings > tap Accounts > Manage accounts > select your Google Account.
Tap Sync account > Menu > Sync now. Allow sync to finish so that your data is up-to-date.
Go back to the previous screen and tap Remove account.
Open WhatsApp to add the previously removed account.
Tap More options > Settings > Chats > Chat backup > Google Account. Alternatively, you can add the account upon installing WhatsApp.
Resolving corrupted backups
If you've tried all the recommended troubleshooting steps and are still unable to restore your backup, your backup is most likely corrupted. Backups can become corrupted due to an interruption in the backup creation process, force-closing WhatsApp, sending or receiving corrupted files, or for other reasons. Unfortunately, WhatsApp can't restore a corrupted backup for you or determine what caused the corruption.
Note:
If these steps don’t solve the issue, check your permission settings for WhatsApp.
If your progress bar shows 100% complete and the notification doesn’t go away, there might still be background processes completing, including media upload or download. Please wait a few hours for the notification to go away.
Partial or incomplete backups can be caused by an unstable internet connection, insufficient storage space on your device, or interruptions during the backup process.
Your Google Account only stores one backup per WhatsApp phone number. Each new backup overwrites the old one, so there’s no way to restore a previous Google Account backup.
If you restore a backup and chats are still missing, you most likely restored a backup file that didn't contain the missing chats. Be sure to manually back up your chats before changing devices or restoring so you have the most up-to-date chat history.
